## Review notes

StillPath works without an account. Journal content is stored locally on the device. AI, translation, analytics and sync are off by default. No sacred text is AI-generated or presented without source metadata. To test Premium, open Settings, choose StillPath Premium, and select either the monthly or annual Sandbox product. Purchase restoration is available on the same screen. Existing journal entries remain accessible after a subscription expires.

## Description

Make space for what matters.

StillPath is a private, inclusive companion for prayer, meditation, gratitude, sacred reading, contemplation and journaling. Create a practice that fits your life—from two quiet minutes to a longer personal routine.

Build your own practice from flexible stages. Use an optional timer, move at your own pace, and reflect only when you want to. Your journal is stored locally by default, with clear controls for app lock, AI, translation, analytics and sync.

StillPath supports one tradition, several traditions, a non-denominational approach, or no stated tradition. It never ranks beliefs, uses guilt-based streaks, or claims spiritual authority.

The free app includes core routines, prayer and meditation sessions, text journaling, reminders and basic history. StillPath Premium adds unlimited routines, voice reflection, translation, advanced search and insights, optional AI support, expanded widgets, sync and advanced export.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in App Store account settings. Journal entries remain accessible if Premium expires.
