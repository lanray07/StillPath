# StillPath privacy policy — product draft

This is a product and engineering draft, not legal advice. Replace placeholders, align it to the final providers and jurisdictions, and obtain legal review before publishing.

StillPath is designed to keep personal spiritual practice private. Journal entries, routines, and practice history are stored on the user's device by default. StillPath does not sell journal information and does not use spiritual or journal content for advertising targeting.

Features that may process data away from the device—such as optional AI reflection, automatic translation, cloud synchronization, or analytics—are disabled until the user chooses to enable them. Before enabling a provider, the app must identify what data is sent, why, to whom, and applicable retention. Sacred text is never silently machine-translated and represented as an official translation.

Users can delete individual entries and recordings, delete all journal data, export their data, and change AI, translation, analytics, widget, and synchronization controls. Contact and controller details, subprocessor list, lawful basis, retention periods, deletion-request process, child-safety position, and international-transfer terms must be added before release.
