StillPath Support

Help with your private spiritual practice, journal, reminders, and StillPath Premium.

Ask a question or request support

Tell us what happened, which device and iOS version you use, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not include private journal entries, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Request StillPath support

Support requests are reviewed through the public StillPath issue tracker. If your question contains personal information, describe only the general problem and ask for a private follow-up channel.

Subscriptions and purchases

Restore a purchase
Open StillPath, go to Settings → StillPath Premium, then tap Restore Purchases.
Manage or cancel
Open the iOS Settings app, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Apple manages billing and cancellations.
After Premium ends
Your existing journal entries remain accessible. Premium-only creation and enhancement features become unavailable.

Privacy and troubleshooting

Where is journal content stored?
Journal content is stored locally on your device by default. AI, translation, analytics, and sync are off by default.
The app is not responding as expected
Update StillPath and iOS, close and reopen the app, and restart your device. If the issue continues, send the steps to reproduce it through the support link above.
Important data
Use Settings → Export Journal before deleting the app or erasing your device.
