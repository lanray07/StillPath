Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Private by default

StillPath stores journal entries, practice routines, completed-session history, preferences, and voice recordings locally on your device. Core use does not require an account.

StillPath does not sell personal information and does not use spiritual or journal content for advertising.

Optional features

AI processing, translation, analytics, synchronization, and private widget text are off by default. The current release does not send journal content to an AI or translation provider. If an optional external provider is introduced later, StillPath will identify what data is sent and request permission before processing it away from the device.

Microphone and biometrics

Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record a voice reflection. Recordings are stored in the app's protected local storage. Face ID or Touch ID is used only when you enable app lock; biometric information is handled by iOS and is not accessible to StillPath.

Your choices

You can delete individual journal entries and recordings, delete all journal and practice data, export journal entries, and change privacy controls in StillPath Settings. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data unless you have separately exported it.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email lanraybanks@gmail.com. Please do not include private journal content, passwords, or payment information.
